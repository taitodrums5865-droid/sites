Free downloads: deadline calendar, CSV and embed

Free, no sign-up: the deadlines of open federal small-business set-aside contract opportunities as a calendar you can subscribe to, a CSV file, and a small widget for your own website. Everything is rebuilt every day from SAM.gov Contract Opportunities (U.S. General Services Administration).

Calendar feeds (.ics)

  • All open deadlines (2,939)
  • Deadlines in the next 30 days (2,458)

To subscribe: in Google Calendar choose Other calendars, then From URL, and paste the link. On iPhone go to Settings, Calendar, Accounts, Add Subscribed Calendar. In Outlook choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web. Downloading the file instead adds a one-time copy that will not update.

CSV download

open-deadlines.csv lists every open opportunity in our copy (2,939 rows) with title, organization, category, posting date, deadline (UTC and local), maximum amount where given, the official link and our page. UTF-8, one row per opportunity, rebuilt daily.

Embed on your website

Libraries, small business centers, associations and grant writers can show the next 10 deadlines on their own pages. Paste the code below. It is free, needs no permission, and shows no ads. You may edit or remove the credit line.
