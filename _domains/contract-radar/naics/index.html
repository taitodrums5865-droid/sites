Federal Contracts by NAICS Sector

Agencies tag every notice with a NAICS code for the industry. Pick a sector to see open opportunities, then drill down to 6-digit codes:

  • Construction (NAICS 23) (347)
  • Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services (NAICS 54) (221)
  • Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services (NAICS 56) (138)
  • Other Services (except Public Administration) (NAICS 81) (54)
  • Transportation and Warehousing (NAICS 48-49) (49)
  • Information (NAICS 51) (37)
  • Health Care and Social Assistance (NAICS 62) (36)
  • Real Estate and Rental and Leasing (NAICS 53) (32)
  • Accommodation and Food Services (NAICS 72) (16)
  • Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing and Hunting (NAICS 11) (16)

  • Public Administration (NAICS 92) (5)
  • Arts, Entertainment, and Recreation (NAICS 71) (4)
  • Mining, Quarrying, and Oil and Gas Extraction (NAICS 21) (3)
