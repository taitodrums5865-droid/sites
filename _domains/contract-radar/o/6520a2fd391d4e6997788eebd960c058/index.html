What this is

A presolicitation is an early notice that the agency plans to issue a solicitation. You usually cannot bid yet, but you can prepare, follow the notice and sometimes send questions or interest.

Who can bid

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) Set-Aside. Reserved for small businesses owned and controlled by service-disabled veterans. Government-wide, this generally requires SBA VetCert certification.

Deadline

2 days left: responses are due Oct 23, 2026, 10:00 AM (UTC-05:00) (as of 2026-10-21). Late responses are usually rejected; check the notice for time zone and submission method.

Next steps

  1. Make sure your business has an active SAM.gov registration — it is free, and you should register yourself on SAM.gov. Never pay anyone for "registration".
  2. Open the official notice, download the attachments and read the statement of work and instructions.
  3. Check that your business fits the NAICS code (561210) and any set-aside requirements.
  4. Send questions before the question deadline (if any), then submit your response exactly as instructed.
