What this is

A combined synopsis/solicitation is a request for quotes or offers published in one notice, common for commercial products and services. You can respond by the deadline.

Who can bid

Veteran-Owned Small Business Set-Aside (VA). Used by the Department of Veterans Affairs for veteran-owned small businesses. Check the notice for the exact verification required.

Deadline

2 days left: responses are due Oct 21, 2026, 3:00 PM (UTC-04:00) (as of 2026-10-19). Late responses are usually rejected; check the notice for time zone and submission method.

Next steps

  1. Make sure your business has an active SAM.gov registration — it is free, and you should register yourself on SAM.gov. Never pay anyone for "registration".
  2. Open the official notice, download the attachments and read the statement of work and instructions.
  3. Check that your business fits the NAICS code (561730) and any set-aside requirements.
  4. Send questions before the question deadline (if any), then submit your response exactly as instructed.
