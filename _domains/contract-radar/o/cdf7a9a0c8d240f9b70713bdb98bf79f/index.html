What this is

A combined synopsis/solicitation is a request for quotes or offers published in one notice, common for commercial products and services. You can respond by the deadline.

Who can bid

No small-business set-aside is listed (No Set aside used), so any responsible business that meets the notice's requirements can usually respond. Small businesses can still compete.

Deadline

4 days left: responses are due Oct 23, 2026, 3:00 PM (UTC-04:00) (as of 2026-10-19). Late responses are usually rejected; check the notice for time zone and submission method.

Next steps

  1. Make sure your business has an active SAM.gov registration — it is free, and you should register yourself on SAM.gov. Never pay anyone for "registration".

  3. Check that your business fits the NAICS code (811210) and any set-aside requirements.
