What this is

A solicitation is a formal request for bids or proposals. If you are eligible, you can respond by the deadline following the instructions in the notice and its attachments.

Who can bid

Small Business Set-Aside. Only small businesses can compete. "Small" is defined by the SBA size standard for the NAICS code on the notice (a revenue or employee limit). A partial set-aside reserves only part of the work for small businesses.

Deadline

4 days left: responses are due Oct 23, 2026, 10:00 AM (UTC-04:00) (as of 2026-10-19). Late responses are usually rejected; check the notice for time zone and submission method.

Next steps

  1. Make sure your business has an active SAM.gov registration — it is free, and you should register yourself on SAM.gov. Never pay anyone for "registration".
  2. Open the official notice, download the attachments and read the statement of work and instructions.
  3. Check that your business fits the NAICS code (238210) and any set-aside requirements.
  4. Send questions before the question deadline (if any), then submit your response exactly as instructed.
