Small Business Set-Aside Contracts by Program

A set-aside reserves a contract for a group of small businesses, so you compete against fewer and similar-sized firms. Pick a program:

  • Small Business Set-Aside (1105)
  • 8(a) Set-Aside (38)
  • Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB / EDWOSB) Set-Aside (39)
  • Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) Set-Aside (236)
  • HUBZone Set-Aside (33)
  • Veteran-Owned Small Business Set-Aside (VA) (1)
