WOSB & EDWOSB Set-Aside Contracts: Open Federal Bids (Nov 2026)

As of 2026-11-12, 30 open federal contract opportunities are listed here: 21 close within 14 days and 30 (100%) are set aside for small businesses. The busiest buyer is Defense Logistics Agency (16) and the most common place of performance is District of Columbia (2).

These notices are reserved under the Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB / EDWOSB) program. Who can bid: Reserved for small businesses certified by the SBA as women-owned (WOSB) or economically disadvantaged women-owned (EDWOSB). The program applies only to certain NAICS codes. Always confirm eligibility in the official notice.
