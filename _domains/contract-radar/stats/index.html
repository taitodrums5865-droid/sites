How these numbers are counted

  • Source: SAM.gov Contract Opportunities (U.S. General Services Administration). U.S. government work; public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105).
  • We copy the public data once a day and count what is in our copy on Oct 22, 2026. This is not an official statistic and can differ from the source's own totals (for example when notices are amended, withdrawn or posted late).
  • "Open" means the deadline has not passed, or no deadline is listed yet. "Newly posted" uses the posting date in the source.
  • For context, the full SAM.gov daily extract had 11,748 active notices, 3,300 of them small-business set-asides; our list keeps the 3,000 set-aside notices it can show in detail.
  • The page is rebuilt every day; the week-on-week table compares with the closest count at least seven days older.
