Federal Bid Deadline Calculator: Business Days Left and a Back-Planned Schedule

Pick an open notice or enter a response deadline. The calculator shows how many business days you have left, skipping weekends and federal holidays, and back-plans draft, review and submission dates. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Example (calculated Sat, Oct 31, 2026): the notice 1/4 INCH VPPP due Mon, Nov 16, 2026 is 10 business days away (16 calendar days). A back-planned schedule: Draft complete by Fri, Nov 6, 2026; Pricing and final review done by Tue, Nov 10, 2026; Target submission day by Fri, Nov 13, 2026.

How the calculation works

Business days are counted from tomorrow up to and including the deadline day. Weekends and the federal holidays above are skipped. The milestones are counted back from the deadline in business days: draft 5, final review 3, submission 1. They are suggestions, not requirements. Always follow the dates, time zone and instructions in the official notice. This is not legal advice. New to bidding? Read how to bid in 6 steps.

FAQ

How many days do agencies usually give to respond?

In our data from 2026-10-31, the median gap between posting and the response deadline was 15 calendar days across 2007 open notices. It varies a lot by notice type; see the table above.

Does the calculator count federal holidays?

Yes. It skips Saturdays, Sundays and the 11 federal holidays in 5 U.S.C. 6103, moved to Friday or Monday when they fall on a weekend. Agencies can still set deadlines on any day; the notice on SAM.gov is final.

Is the back-planned schedule a rule?

No. The draft, review and submission dates are planning buffers we suggest so that you are not uploading at the last minute. Question deadlines, page limits and the submission method are set in each solicitation.

Why is the deadline time important?

SAM.gov deadlines include a time and time zone (often 2 p.m. or 5 p.m. local time of the contracting office). This calculator works in whole days, so plan to submit at least a day early.
