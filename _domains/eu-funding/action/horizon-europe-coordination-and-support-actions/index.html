Horizon Europe Coordination and Support Actions: Open EU Calls (2026)

As of 2026-10-17, 48 EU funding calls are listed here: 10 open for submission and 38 forthcoming; 1 has a deadline within 30 days. Stated budgets add up to €658.9 million; the median is €5 million (47 of 48 state a budget).

Calls whose type of action is Horizon Europe Coordination and Support Actions. Coordination and support actions fund networking, coordination, dissemination, policy support and similar accompanying measures, rather than research itself.

How many calls are listed here?
