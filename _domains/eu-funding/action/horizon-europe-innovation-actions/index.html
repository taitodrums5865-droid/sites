Horizon Europe Innovation Actions: Open EU Calls (2026)

As of 2026-10-30, 119 EU funding calls are listed here: 34 open for submission and 85 forthcoming; 11 have a deadline within 30 days. Stated budgets add up to €2299.5 million; the median is €13.8 million (118 of 119 state a budget).

Calls whose type of action is Horizon Europe Innovation Actions. Innovation actions fund activities closer to market, such as prototyping, testing, demonstration and piloting. They usually need a consortium of partners from several countries.

FAQ

How many calls are listed here?

119 (34 open, 85 forthcoming), based on EU Funding & Tenders Portal data retrieved 2026-10-30. The portal itself is the official and complete list.

Which deadline comes first?

Accessible and available travel facilitation (HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-02) — next deadline 5 Nov 2026.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Creating an EU Login, registering your organisation and submitting a proposal on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal is free. Be wary of anyone charging for access to calls.
