Type of action

This is a cascade funding call: an EU-funded project (SBEP) passes on part of its EU grant to third parties. You usually apply on the project's own website, which is linked from the official page, and the rules are set in the project's call.

Deadline

Deadline as published: 16 Nov 2026. Check the official page for the exact time and any changes; late submissions are not accepted.
