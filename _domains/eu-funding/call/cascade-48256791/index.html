European City Facility – Eighth Call for Applications: Support local authorities in developing investment concepts for sustainable energy

European City Facility – Eighth Call for Applications: Support local authorities in developing investment concepts for sustainable energy (LIFE-2025-CET-EUCF) is a forthcoming cascade funding call under LIFE with a stated budget of €3.7 million. The next deadline is 21 Dec 2026, 59 days away as of 2026-10-23. It is planned to open on 26 Oct 2026.

Type of action

This is a cascade funding call: an EU-funded project (LIFE25-CET-LIFE EUCF 3.0) passes on part of its EU grant to third parties. You usually apply on the project's own website, which is linked from the official page, and the rules are set in the project's call.

Deadline

Deadline as published: 21 Dec 2026. Check the official page for the exact time and any changes; late submissions are not accepted.

Next steps

  1. Open the official page and follow the link to the project's call page.
  2. Read the project's guide for applicants: who can apply, how much you can get and what you must deliver.
  3. Prepare and submit your application in the project's own system before the deadline.
