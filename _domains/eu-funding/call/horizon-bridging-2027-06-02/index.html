Type of action

Research and innovation actions fund new knowledge or the feasibility of a new technology, product or service. They usually need a consortium of partners from several countries.

Deadline

Deadline as published: 16 Feb 2027. Check the official page for the exact time and any changes; late submissions are not accepted.

Next steps

  1. Open the official topic page and read the conditions, the call document and the evaluation criteria.
  2. Create a free EU Login and register your organisation to get a PIC, if you do not have one.
  3. Find partners if the topic needs a consortium (the portal's partner search is free).
  4. Start a draft in the submission system from the topic page and submit before the deadline.
