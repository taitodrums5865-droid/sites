New primitives and protocols for functionalities of future hybrid classical-hybrid and quantum networks

New primitives and protocols for functionalities of future hybrid classical-hybrid and quantum networks (HORIZON-CL3-2027-02-CS-ECCC-04) is a forthcoming funding topic under Horizon Europe with a stated budget of €4 million. The next deadline is 15 Sep 2027, 325 days away as of 2026-10-25. It is planned to open on 2 Mar 2027.
