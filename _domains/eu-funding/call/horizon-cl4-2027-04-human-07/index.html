Plain-English summary

Fund a coordination support action to increase European influence in global digital standards-setting bodies. Financially support participation of European experts—particularly from SMEs, startups, research institutions, open-source communities, and civil society—in technical committees and standards development. Map standardisation gaps and emerging technology needs across key digital domains. Raise awareness among industry, especially SMEs and researchers, of standardisation benefits for competitive advantage and tech sovereignty. Host international standards meetings in Europe to ease participation. Develop skills training, educational materials, and foresight analyses on emerging standards needs.

Who it fits: Intermediary organizations supporting standards participation: associations, networks, research bodies, NGOs. Complete eligibility rules on official page.

  • Single award of €7 million; management facility for expert support and chairing roles mandatory
  • Gap analysis required for key digital priority areas from Annual Union Work Programme; include foresight on emerging tech
  • Build on previous StandICT.eu work and coordinate with national-level initiatives
