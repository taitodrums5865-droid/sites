Increasing knowledge flows to practice within AKIS via EU thematic knowledge hubs

Increasing knowledge flows to practice within AKIS via EU thematic knowledge hubs (HORIZON-CL6-2027-03-GOVERNANCE-05) is a forthcoming funding topic under Horizon Europe with a stated budget of €7 million. The next deadline is 11 May 2027, 204 days away as of 2026-10-18. It is planned to open on 4 Feb 2027.
