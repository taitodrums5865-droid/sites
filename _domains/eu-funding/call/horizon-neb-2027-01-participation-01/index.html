The transformative potential of co-governance in the green transition of neighbourhoods

The transformative potential of co-governance in the green transition of neighbourhoods (HORIZON-NEB-2027-01-PARTICIPATION-01) is a forthcoming funding topic under Horizon Europe with a stated budget of €10.8 million. The next deadline is 1 Dec 2027, 408 days away as of 2026-10-19. It is planned to open on 5 May 2027.

In our daily copy of EU Funding & Tenders Portal data (2026-10-19), 9 other topics in call HORIZON-NEB-2027-01 are listed; Horizon Europe has 376 open or forthcoming calls here; the median stated budget for Horizon Europe calls is €12 million; 18 Horizon Europe calls have a deadline in December 2027.

Type of action

Innovation actions fund activities closer to market, such as prototyping, testing, demonstration and piloting. They usually need a consortium of partners from several countries.

Deadline

Deadline as published: 1 Dec 2027. Check the official page for the exact time and any changes; late submissions are not accepted.
