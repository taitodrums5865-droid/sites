Combining conventional and innovative construction materials, products and techniques for sustainable and beautiful buildings

Combining conventional and innovative construction materials, products and techniques for sustainable and beautiful buildings (HORIZON-NEB-2027-01-REGEN-01) is a forthcoming funding topic under Horizon Europe with a stated budget of €12 million. The next deadline is 1 Dec 2027, 409 days away as of 2026-10-17. It is planned to open on 5 May 2027.

Type of action
