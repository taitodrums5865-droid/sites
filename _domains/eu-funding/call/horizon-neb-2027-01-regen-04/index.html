Enabling environments for a sustainable and circular construction sector

Enabling environments for a sustainable and circular construction sector (HORIZON-NEB-2027-01-REGEN-04) is a forthcoming funding topic under Horizon Europe with a stated budget of €9 million. The next deadline is 1 Dec 2027, 404 days away as of 2026-10-23. It is planned to open on 5 May 2027.

Type of action

Innovation actions fund activities closer to market, such as prototyping, testing, demonstration and piloting. They usually need a consortium of partners from several countries.

Deadline

Deadline as published: 1 Dec 2027. Check the official page for the exact time and any changes; late submissions are not accepted.

Next steps

  1. Open the official topic page and read the conditions, the call document and the evaluation criteria.

  4. Start a draft in the submission system from the topic page and submit before the deadline.
