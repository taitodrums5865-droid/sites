Information measures for the EU Cohesion policy for 2026

Information measures for the EU Cohesion policy for 2026 (IMREG-2026-INFOME) is a forthcoming funding topic under Information measures (IMREG) with a stated budget of €3.5 million. The next deadline is 13 Jan 2027, 88 days away as of 2026-10-17. It is planned to open on 14 Oct 2026.
