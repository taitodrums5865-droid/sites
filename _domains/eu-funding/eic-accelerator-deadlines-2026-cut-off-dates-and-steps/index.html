EIC Accelerator Deadlines: 2026 Cut-Off Dates and Steps

The EIC Accelerator deadlines for 2026 are six full-proposal cut-offs: 7 January, 4 March, 6 May, 8 July, 2 September and 4 November 2026, each at 17:00 Brussels time (EIC Accelerator page). You can submit a short application at any time. Short applications are collected into a batch for evaluation on the first Tuesday of every month. As of 21 October 2026, the only cut-off left this year is 4 November, and you can only submit to it if your short application has already received a "GO".

Key points

  • Six 2026 full-proposal cut-offs; only 4 November 2026 is left
  • Short applications are batched on the first Tuesday of each month
  • Draft 2027 plan cuts this to four cut-offs, starting 3 February

Best for: Deep-tech start-ups, SMEs and grant consultants planning EIC bids

What Are the EIC Accelerator Deadlines for 2026?

The 2026 EIC Accelerator has six fixed full-proposal cut-offs and a short application that stays open all year. The EIC moved to a schedule of one cut-off every two months for 2026. This replaced the two cut-offs it ran in 2025.

These dates come from the official EIC Accelerator page and the Cyprus national funding portal's summary of call HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01. Status is as of October 2026:

What Are the EIC Accelerator Deadlines for 2026?. Source: EU Funding & Tenders Portal
Full-proposal cut-off (17:00 Brussels)Status on 21 Oct 2026

4 March 2026Closed
6 May 2026Closed
8 July 2026Closed

4 November 2026Open, and the last cut-off of 2026

According to the Cyprus portal, if your short application succeeds under the 2026 Work Programme, you can send your full proposal to any of these 2026 dates. The call opened on 6 November 2025. Its budget is €634 million: €414 million for EIC Accelerator Open and €220 million for EIC Accelerator Challenges (Cyprus portal).

Pitfalls with the 2026 dates

  • Third-party sites show different "deadline" dates. Aggregators list different closing dates for the same call ID. One shows 17 December, another shows 4 November (euacc.ai, Cyprus portal). To fix this, trust only the deadline shown on the official topic page and the batching dates in the Work Programme PDF.
  • Time zone. The 17:00 cut-off is Brussels time (CET/CEST). For example, a team in Lisbon or Dublin has until 16:00 local time. A team in Helsinki has until 18:00.
  • Interviews follow every second cut-off, not every one. Jury interview sessions are organised after every second full-proposal batch, and tentative dates are posted on the EIC website (Cyprus portal). Keep that period free in the team's calendar.

What Are the EIC Accelerator Deadlines for 2027?

The 2027 EIC Accelerator dates are only in draft: 3 February, 1 April, 1 September and 3 November 2027. A draft of the EIC Work Programme 2027 dated 7 July 2026 lists these four full-proposal cut-offs. That draft is labelled as not adopted or endorsed by the European Commission (evolution-europe summary of the draft). As of October 2026, no adopted 2027 programme turned up in the sources checked. So treat these dates as planning estimates, not commitments.

The draft makes three changes that affect planning (evolution-europe, Myriad Associates, 24 Aug 2026):

  1. The number of cut-offs falls from six to four. Missing April 2027 would mean waiting five months for the next one.
  2. A new "GO Reserve" list lets the jury hold strong near-misses. They could be funded if budget is left over after the year's selections (Rasph summary).
  3. Challenge topics fall from five to four. The draft sets €150 million across climate-neutral and smart cities (€50m), carbon removal and circularity (€50m), in-situ ocean observation (€30m) and fusion power plants (€20m).

Pitfall: don't make investor or hiring commitments that depend on the draft dates. Fix: plan for an April 2027 submission and keep September 2027 as the fallback. Recheck once the Commission announces adoption. The 2026 programme's adoption was announced on the Commission press corner, so watch there.

What Were the EIC Accelerator Deadlines for 2025?

The 2025 EIC Accelerator had only two full-proposal cut-offs: 12 March 2025 and 1 October 2025. Short applications were batched on the first Tuesday of each month, as they are now (ERRIN call listing, Segler Consulting). Both dates are closed, and a 2025 GO does not entitle you to submit under 2026 rules automatically. Check the eligibility section of the 2026 Work Programme before you rely on an older result.

For example, say a short application goes into the 3 November batch and gets results after four to six weeks. That means early-to-mid December 2026. By then the last 2026 cut-off has passed, so the realistic full-proposal target becomes the first 2027 cut-off. Whether a GO received in late 2026 can be used for a 2027 cut-off depends on the transition rules in the adopted 2027 programme. Confirm this with your National Contact Point rather than assuming it.

How Does EIC Accelerator Submission Deadlines Work (Step-by-Step)?

You can submit a short application at any time. A GO lets you submit a full proposal by a fixed cut-off, and that is followed by remote evaluation and then a jury interview. Every proposal goes through the Funding & Tenders Portal, so start with your organisation's registration.

Steps from zero to full proposal

  1. Get a PIC. On the Funding & Tenders Portal, log in with your EU Login and register your organisation to receive a 9-digit Participant Identification Code. Our PIC registration guide walks through all six screens. If you're a natural person who hasn't founded the company yet, you can still apply (EIC).
  2. Open the topic. In the Portal, go to Search Funding & Tenders, search for HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01, and open the topic page.
  3. Pick Open or Challenge. Read the Challenge topics in the 2026 Work Programme. If your innovation fits one, the Challenges budget (€220m) is competing for a narrower field than Open (€414m).
  4. Start the short proposal. On the topic page, use Start submission, enter your PIC, and complete the 12-page short form. Upload a pitch deck of up to 10 slides as a PDF, and a video pitch of up to 3 minutes.
  5. Submit before the first Tuesday of the month, 17:00 Brussels. That puts your proposal in the month's batch. Expect a GO or NO-GO within 4–6 weeks.
  6. After a GO, build the full proposal. You need the 20-page form, an updated pitch deck, the implementation plan, financial information, customer or partner Letters of Intent, a freedom-to-operate (FTO) analysis and a new 3-minute video (EIC).
  7. Submit to a cut-off. Choose one of the batching dates and submit before 17:00 Brussels. The proposal is reviewed remotely by EIC experts, with results in 8–9 weeks.
  8. Prepare for the interview. If you're shortlisted, you pitch to the EIC Jury. Results arrive 2–3 weeks after the interview week.

Use the Horizon Europe Part B scoring map for general guidance on writing to the evaluation criteria. Keep in mind that the EIC uses its own templates and does not use the standard Part B.

What Is the EIC Pre-Accelerator and Its Deadline?

The EIC Pre-Accelerator is a separate, earlier-stage grant for deep-tech start-ups in Horizon Europe Widening countries. It has no 2026 call, and a 2027 call is expected. It is run jointly by the EIC and the Horizon Europe WIDERA programme. Its only deadline so far was 18 November 2025 (CORDIS: HORIZON-WIDERA-2025-02-ACCESS-01, Opportunity Desk). It is a Funding & Tenders Portal call, not an Enterprise Europe Network bootcamp. People often confuse it with national "pre-accelerator" programmes.

On the 2026 gap, Catalyze states that "there is no Pre-Accelerator in 2026, but it is expected to return in 2027". It lists an indicative 2027 window of 5 May to 18 November 2027, €500k–€1m per project and a €40m budget (Catalyze). The draft EIC 2027 Work Programme itself contains no Pre-Accelerator section (evolution-europe). The call sits under WIDERA, so watch the WIDERA work programme, not the EIC one.

Pitfall: applying to the Accelerator at TRL 4 because the Pre-Accelerator is closed. Fix: use the time to reach TRL 5 and gather customer evidence. If you're in a Widening country, prepare for the expected 2027 Pre-Accelerator call.

How Much Is the EIC Accelerator Grant and When Is It Due?

The 2026 EIC Accelerator grant is up to €2.5 million, and investment of €1–10 million is available on top. You apply for both through the same short application and full-proposal cut-offs. The grant is a lump sum for TRL 6–8 activities completed within 24 months (EIC).

If a guide you're reading quotes a different equity ceiling, check its date against the official page. For 2026, the official range is €1–10 million per company in the Accelerator itself (as of October 2026, EIC). Larger amounts go through STEP Scale-Up, which is a separate call. One consultancy reports that a separate EIC STEP Defence Scale Up call offers up to €30 million and closes on 28 October 2026 (One Synergy). Check that call's own topic page before relying on it.

Payment timing: the grant is not paid at the cut-off. The steps are evaluation (8–9 weeks), the interview (results in 2–3 weeks), grant preparation and then the first payment, so plan cash flow accordingly. Applicants who pass the thresholds but aren't funded receive a Seal of Excellence, which some national programmes accept (EIC).

For example, a €3m project budget under grant-only would receive at most €2.1m (70%). The company would have to fund €0.9m itself. Show where that 30% comes from in the financial information.

Is the EIC Accelerator Open Right Now?

Yes, the EIC Accelerator is open: short applications are accepted at any time, and the 4 November 2026 full-proposal cut-off is still ahead. Only teams that already hold a GO can use the November cut-off.

To check the live status yourself:

  1. Open the topic page for HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01.
  2. Look at the Topic status label (Open / Forthcoming / Closed) and the deadline field in the General information box.
  3. Compare with the batching dates on the EIC Accelerator page.
  4. Once November passes, search the Portal for HORIZON-EIC-2027-ACCELERATOR. Aggregators already mention a 2027 call ID (euacc.ai), but only an official topic page confirms that it exists.

If you use the Portal's search filters, the same filter tips from our cascade funding guide apply, especially filtering by Open for submission.

Next steps

Today, choose your target cut-off and plan backwards from it.

Do-it-yourself actions for today

  1. Check that your organisation has a validated PIC. If it doesn't, follow the PIC registration guide today, because validation can take time.
  2. If you have no GO yet, draft the 12-page short form, the 10-slide deck and the 3-minute video. Aim for the 3 November 2026 batch at 17:00 Brussels, or 1 December if that's too tight.
  3. Download the 2026 Work Programme PDF and read the Challenge annexes to decide between Open and Challenge.

If you'd rather not do it alone

  • National Contact Points (NCPs): free, government-funded advisers for Horizon Europe in each EU and Associated country. They're a good fit if you want rule checks and a proposal pre-read. They don't fit if you need someone to write the proposal for you, because they won't. Capacity varies by country.
  • Enterprise Europe Network (EEN): free support for SMEs (as of October 2026; check the official page). EEN is useful for partner search and local guidance. It is not a dedicated proposal-writing service.
  • Private EIC consultancies: before you sign, ask each provider for its full fee structure, including any success fee, and what happens after a NO-GO, and get it in writing. A consultancy suits teams with funding but no writing time. It doesn't suit first-time applicants who haven't validated their own market story yet, and no consultant can guarantee a GO.

FAQ

What is the EIC Accelerator open deadline?

There is no single closing date for short applications. They are open all year and batched on the first Tuesday of each month at 17:00 Brussels time (EIC). Full proposals have fixed cut-offs, and the last one in 2026 is 4 November.

What are the EIC Accelerator dates for 2026?

The full-proposal cut-offs are 7 January, 4 March, 6 May, 8 July, 2 September and 4 November 2026, all at 17:00 Brussels time. Interview sessions follow every second batch (Cyprus portal).

What are the EIC Accelerator deadlines for 2027?

A draft 2027 Work Programme lists 3 February, 1 April, 1 September and 3 November 2027. The draft has not been adopted, so the dates could change (evolution-europe). Check the Funding & Tenders Portal once the 2027 call is published.

Is there an EIC Pre-Accelerator deadline in 2026?

No. The pilot call closed on 18 November 2025, and sources say a new call is expected in 2027 (CORDIS, Catalyze). It is limited to start-ups in Widening countries at TRL 4 or higher.
