FAQ

How many calls are listed here?

12 (7 open, 5 forthcoming), based on EU Funding & Tenders Portal data retrieved 2026-10-19. The portal itself is the official and complete list.

Which deadline comes first?

Preventing gender-based violence (CERV-2026-DAPHNE-PREVENTION) — next deadline 5 Nov 2026.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Creating an EU Login, registering your organisation and submitting a proposal on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal is free. Be wary of anyone charging for access to calls.
