How the finder works

It filters the same data as the lists on this site: programme, type of action, status, the next deadline and the expected EU contribution per project from the call text. It cannot check eligibility rules such as consortium size or country. Read the topic conditions on the portal before you start; see how to apply for EU funding.

FAQ

How many EU funding calls are open right now?

In our data from 2026-10-30, 220 calls are open for submission and 253 more are forthcoming; 69 open calls have a deadline in the next 30 days.

Which EU programme has the most calls?

Horizon Europe, with 126 open and 245 forthcoming calls in our data.

What is the EU contribution per project?

The amount the call text says the EU expects to give one project (often a range). It is not your guaranteed grant, and many calls also need co-funding. Where the portal lists no figure, we leave it blank.

Can I apply alone?

It depends on the call. Many Horizon Europe actions need a consortium of partners from several countries. The eligibility conditions are in the topic page on the portal. This finder does not give legal advice.
