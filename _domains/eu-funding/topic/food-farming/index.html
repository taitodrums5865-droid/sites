FAQ

How many calls are listed here?

58 (13 open, 45 forthcoming), based on EU Funding & Tenders Portal data retrieved 2026-10-28. The portal itself is the official and complete list.

Which deadline comes first?

Additional activities for the European Partnership of Agriculture of Data (HORIZON-CL6-2026-04-GOVERNANCE-01) — next deadline 26 Nov 2026.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Creating an EU Login, registering your organisation and submitting a proposal on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal is free. Be wary of anyone charging for access to calls.
