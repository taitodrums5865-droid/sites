As of 2026-10-31, 36 EU funding calls are listed here: 14 open for submission and 22 forthcoming; 4 have a deadline within 30 days. Stated budgets add up to €501.2 million; the median is €10.8 million (34 of 36 state a budget). Most come from Horizon Europe (31).

Calls whose title, call or portal keywords mention SMEs, startups, scale-ups or entrepreneurs. Many other calls also accept companies as consortium partners. Matching is by keyword, so check each call's scope.

FAQ

How many calls are listed here?

36 (14 open, 22 forthcoming), based on EU Funding & Tenders Portal data retrieved 2026-10-31. The portal itself is the official and complete list.

Which deadline comes first?

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal (HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01) — next deadline 4 Nov 2026.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Creating an EU Login, registering your organisation and submitting a proposal on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal is free. Be wary of anyone charging for access to calls.
