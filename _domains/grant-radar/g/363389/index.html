Summary (excerpt)

The purpose of this demonstration project is to support coordinated Clinic and Community Collaboratives (CCCs) to integrate HIV care with broader women's health services in jurisdictions with disproportionately high HIV disease burden to improve HIV-related outcomes, overall health, and quality of life among WWH.
