Who it is for

UK small business owners, sole traders, charities and social enterprises (SMEs and VCSEs) looking for public sector contracts they can win.

Guides are researched from official documentation, changelogs and other public sources. Each guide lists its sources and the date it was checked, so you can verify the details yourself.

Editorial policy

  • We research official docs, pricing pages, terms and changelogs, and cite them inline
  • We never invent experiences, tests or results; when something is unknown we say so
  • Recommendations include who it is not for, limitations and cost; affiliate links are disclosed at the top
  • Guides are reviewed and updated regularly (the update date is shown)

How guides are made

Guides on Standstill UK Tenders are bylined Aoba Works, the publisher. They are drafted with AI assistance. The drafting step reads the official documentation, pricing pages and other primary sources and links each factual claim to its source. Before publishing, every draft goes through an automated editorial check that rejects unsourced claims about products, prices or limits, invented experiences or test results, hype and income promises. Publishing is automated; we do not claim hands-on testing.

Older guides are re-checked against current official sources on a rolling schedule; when a guide changes, the update date shown on it changes too. Each guide shows its publication date (and update date) and a short note on how it was made.

Data pages (lists, deadlines and counts) are generated once a day directly from the public data sources named on each page. They are not written by AI.

Advertising and affiliate links

Some guides may contain affiliate links. When a guide does, a disclosure appears at the top of that guide. Commissions never decide what we recommend, and sponsored content is not published as editorial content.
