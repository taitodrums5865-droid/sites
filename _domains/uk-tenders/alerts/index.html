Free alerts by RSS

Every list on Standstill UK Tenders has its own RSS feed. Subscribe to the lists that match your business and new items show up in your reader each day. It is free, and you do not need an account or give us your email.

How to subscribe

  1. Pick a list below (or open any list page and find RSS feed for this list).
  2. Copy the feed address (right-click the link and copy it).
  3. Paste it into an RSS reader. Many readers are free on phone and desktop; email programs such as Thunderbird and team chat tools such as Slack can also follow RSS feeds.

Each feed holds the 50 newest items in that list and is rebuilt once a day from public data. Items link to our summary page, which links to the official notice. Always read the official notice before you apply or bid: dates and documents can change.

What about email alerts?

We do not collect email addresses at the moment. RSS works without sharing any personal information.
