If you bid and lost

  1. Read the assessment summary the authority sent you before the award notice. It must explain how your bid scored against the winner.
  2. Ask questions early. The standstill clock does not stop while you wait for answers.
  3. Take legal advice before the last working day if you think the award is wrong. After the contract is signed, the options shrink.

FAQ

What is the standstill period?

Under section 51 of the Procurement Act 2023, a contracting authority must not enter into a public contract until a mandatory standstill period has ended. It lasts eight working days, beginning with the day the contract award notice is published.

Why does it matter to bidders?

It is the window to ask questions about the award and, if needed, to start a legal challenge before the contract is signed. Once the contract is entered into, the remedies available are much narrower.

Is every award on this list really in standstill?

Not always. Some awards are exempt (for example many call-offs under frameworks, or urgent direct awards), and some authorities apply a voluntary standstill instead. The end date here is an estimate from the notice date. Always check the award notice.
