How these numbers are counted

  • Source: Find a Tender and Contracts Finder (UK government). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • We copy the public data once a day and count what is in our copy on Oct 18, 2026. This is not an official statistic and can differ from the source's own totals (for example when notices are amended, withdrawn or posted late).
  • "Open" means the deadline has not passed, or no deadline is listed yet. "Newly posted" uses the posting date in the source.
  • The page is rebuilt every day; the week-on-week table compares with the closest count at least seven days older.
