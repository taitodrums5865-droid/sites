About Set-Aside

Open U.S. federal contract opportunities for small businesses, sorted by deadline, set-aside, NAICS code and state — with plain-English guides to bidding.

Who it is for

U.S. small business owners, contractors and first-time bidders looking for federal contract opportunities they can win.

Guides are researched from official documentation, changelogs and other public sources. Each guide lists its sources and the date it was checked, so you can verify the details yourself.

Editorial policy

  • We research official docs, pricing pages, terms and changelogs, and cite them inline
  • We never invent experiences, tests or results; when something is unknown we say so
  • Recommendations include who it is not for, limitations and cost; affiliate links are disclosed at the top
  • Guides are reviewed and updated regularly (the update date is shown)

Who runs this site

Set-Aside is published by aobaworks (aobaworks.com), which runs a small group of independent guide and data sites. Data pages are rebuilt from their public sources once a day. Guides are re-checked against their sources on a regular schedule.
