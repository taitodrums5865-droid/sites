What this is

A combined synopsis/solicitation is a request for quotes or offers published in one notice, common for commercial products and services. You can respond by the deadline.

Who can bid

Deadline

13 days left: responses are due Oct 30, 2026, 1:00 PM (UTC-05:00) (as of 2026-10-17). Late responses are usually rejected; check the notice for time zone and submission method.
