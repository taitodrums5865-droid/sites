About Call Window

Open and upcoming EU funding calls from the EU Funding & Tenders Portal, sorted by deadline, programme and type of action — with plain-English guides to applying.

Who it is for

small businesses, startups, researchers, universities, NGOs and grant consultants in Europe and beyond looking for EU funding calls they can apply to.

Guides are researched from official documentation, changelogs and other public sources. Each guide lists its sources and the date it was checked, so you can verify the details yourself.

Editorial policy

  • We research official docs, pricing pages, terms and changelogs, and cite them inline
  • We never invent experiences, tests or results; when something is unknown we say so
  • Recommendations include who it is not for, limitations and cost; affiliate links are disclosed at the top
  • Guides are reviewed and updated regularly (the update date is shown)

Who runs this site

Call Window is published by aobaworks (aobaworks.com), which runs a small group of independent guide and data sites. Guides are re-checked against their sources on a regular schedule.

Data source and reuse

Funding call data comes from the EU Funding & Tenders Portal (European Commission), read once a day through the portal's public search service. We keep facts only: identifiers, titles, programme, opening and deadline dates, budget and type of action, with a link to the official topic page. We do not copy call texts and do not keep personal data.

The portal's legal notice points to the European Commission legal notice, which states (checked 17 October 2026):

“Unless otherwise indicated (e.g. in individual copyright notices), content owned by the EU on this website is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence. This means that reuse is allowed, provided appropriate credit is given and changes are indicated.”

Source: European Commission legal notice, which notes that the Commission's reuse policy is implemented by Commission Decision 2011/833/EU. EU logos and emblems are excluded from the reuse policy, so we do not use them.

Changes we make: we reorganise the data into lists by programme, deadline and type of action, shorten titles for page headings and add counts calculated from the data. Call Window is independent and unofficial; it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the European Commission or any EU agency. Always check the official topic page before you apply.
