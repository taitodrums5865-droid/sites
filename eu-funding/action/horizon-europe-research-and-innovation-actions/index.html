Horizon Europe Research and Innovation Actions: Open EU Calls (2026)

As of 2026-10-17, 145 EU funding calls are listed here: 25 open for submission and 120 forthcoming; 7 have a deadline within 30 days. Stated budgets add up to €2638.7 million; the median is €12 million (143 of 145 state a budget).

Calls whose type of action is Horizon Europe Research and Innovation Actions. Research and innovation actions fund new knowledge or the feasibility of a new technology, product or service. They usually need a consortium of partners from several countries.
