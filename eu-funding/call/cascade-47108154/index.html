RENEW-BOOSTER EUROCLUSTER CALL N°2 FOR FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO THIRD PARTIES

RENEW-BOOSTER EUROCLUSTER CALL N°2 FOR FINANCIAL SUPPORT TO THIRD PARTIES (SMP-COSME-2024-CLUSTER-01) is an open cascade funding call under Single Market Programme with a stated budget of €800,000. The next deadline is 30 Nov 2026, 44 days away as of 2026-10-17.

Type of action

This is a cascade funding call: an EU-funded project (RENEW-BOOSTER) passes on part of its EU grant to third parties. You usually apply on the project's own website, which is linked from the official page, and the rules are set in the project's call.

Deadline

Deadline as published: 30 Nov 2026. Check the official page for the exact time and any changes; late submissions are not accepted.

Next steps

  1. Open the official page and follow the link to the project's call page.
  2. Read the project's guide for applicants: who can apply, how much you can get and what you must deliver.
