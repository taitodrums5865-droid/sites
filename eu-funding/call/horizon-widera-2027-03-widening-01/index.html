ERA Fellowships (HORIZON-WIDERA-2027-03-WIDENING-01) is a forthcoming funding topic under Horizon Europe with a stated budget of €8 million. The next deadline is 8 Sep 2027, 326 days away as of 2026-10-17. It is planned to open on 7 Apr 2027.
