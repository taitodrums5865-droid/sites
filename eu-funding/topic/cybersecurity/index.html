FAQ

How many calls are listed here?

20 (13 open, 7 forthcoming), based on EU Funding & Tenders Portal data retrieved 2026-10-17. The portal itself is the official and complete list.

Which deadline comes first?

Cyber Security Boost: Support for the development, pilot testing and wider adoption of solutions for cyber resilience. (DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-NCC-06-MS-COORDINATION) — next deadline 30 Nov 2026.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Creating an EU Login, registering your organisation and submitting a proposal on the EU Funding & Tenders Portal is free. Be wary of anyone charging for access to calls.
