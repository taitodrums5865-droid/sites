About First Grade

Federal internships and entry-level jobs open now for students and recent grads, searchable by closing date, GS grade, state and agency, updated daily from USAJOBS data.

Who it is for

U.S. high school, college and trade-school students and recent graduates who want their first paid federal internship or entry-level government job but find USAJOBS hard to search and understand.

Guides are researched from official documentation, changelogs and other public sources. Each guide lists its sources and the date it was checked, so you can verify the details yourself.

Editorial policy

  • We research official docs, pricing pages, terms and changelogs, and cite them inline
  • We never invent experiences, tests or results; when something is unknown we say so
  • Recommendations include who it is not for, limitations and cost; affiliate links are disclosed at the top
  • Guides are reviewed and updated regularly (the update date is shown)

Who runs this site

First Grade is published by aobaworks (aobaworks.com). Guides are re-checked against their sources on a regular schedule.
