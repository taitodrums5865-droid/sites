About Outlay

Open U.S. federal grants, sorted by deadline, amount and who can apply — with plain-English guides to applying.

Who it is for

U.S. small business owners, nonprofits, researchers and first-time applicants looking for federal grants.

Guides are researched from official documentation, changelogs and other public sources. Each guide lists its sources and the date it was checked, so you can verify the details yourself.

Editorial policy

  • We research official docs, pricing pages, terms and changelogs, and cite them inline
  • We never invent experiences, tests or results; when something is unknown we say so
  • Recommendations include who it is not for, limitations and cost; affiliate links are disclosed at the top
  • Guides are reviewed and updated regularly (the update date is shown)
