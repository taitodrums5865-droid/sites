How this procurement works

This uses the competitive flexible procedure (Procurement Act 2023). The buyer designs its own stages, often a request to participate first, then invited tenders. Read the notice for each stage and deadline.

Who can bid

The notice is not marked as suitable for SMEs or VCSEs. Small businesses can still bid if they meet the requirements, or join a consortium or a prime contractor's supply chain.

Closing date

6 days left: the deadline for requests to participate is 23 Oct 2026, 17:00 UK time (as of 2026-10-17). Late submissions are normally rejected; check the notice for the exact time and how to submit.

Next steps
